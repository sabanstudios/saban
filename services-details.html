Handcrafted Elegance & Creative Group Experiences

At Saban Craft, we transform quality beads, natural stones, and durable cords into unique wearable art. Whether you are looking for a personalized phone charm that matches your aesthetic or want to host a live craft booth for a birthday party, pop-up market, or corporate retreat, we provide hands-on expertise and all high-quality raw materials.

  • Custom Design Consultation: Select your preferred bead themes, colors, initials, and charm accents.
  • Durable Crafting Standards: High-tensile cords and secure loop finishing ensure your phone charms last.
  • Guided On-Site Workshops: Full setup and step-by-step instruction for birthday parties and social gatherings.

Our interactive sessions guide participants through color coordination, pattern building, knotting techniques, and finishing touches. Every guest walks away with a finished, custom-built phone charm or accessory they created themselves.

We regularly showcase our work and host artisan sessions across popular Nairobi locations, including Sarit Centre, Kneads Bakery & Cafe, and pop-up events at The Alchemist in Westlands. For custom orders or event bookings, reach out directly through our contact details or book a session online.
